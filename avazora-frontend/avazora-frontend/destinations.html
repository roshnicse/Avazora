Plan Your Sacred Visit

Key essentials and practical recommendations to make your temple pilgrimage seamless and reverent.

🗓️

Best Time

October to March offers mild, pleasant weather for temple parikramas and festival celebrations across South and North India. Himalayan shrines welcome pilgrims during May to October.

✈️

How to Reach

Key sacred hubs feature direct flight connections via Chennai, Madurai, Dehradun, and Cochin, supplemented by dedicated Vande Bharat trains and sanitised AC yatra coaches.

🏨

Stay Options

Choose from verified devalayam trust cottages and peaceful ashrams to premium heritage resorts offering sattvic culinary dining close to sanctum sanctorums.

🧭

Travel Tips

Adhere to traditional dress codes (dhoti/saree/kurta), book special darshan tokens early through Avazora, remove footwear at designated stands, and carry valid photo IDs.
